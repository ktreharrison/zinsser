# Operational document review

Use this guidance for high- and low-level designs, runbooks, SOPs, and service-transition documents. Zinsser checks prose and cadence; it does not validate technical correctness or operational completeness.

When editing procedures, preserve exact commands, order, warnings, owners, prerequisites, rollback steps, and verification criteria. Do not shorten or restructure a step if doing so could change execution or responsibility. Flag missing or ambiguous information for the owner; do not invent it.

For design and transition documents, make decisions, dependencies, owners, risks, and acceptance criteria easy to find. Treat completeness as a question for the responsible technical or service owner, not as something a prose lint pass can certify.
